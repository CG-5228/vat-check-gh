## Maintenance

Keep `data/aliases.json` and `data/alias-evidence.json` together. Review source changes before advancing the review date. Primary company pages may show a different regional contracting party; preserve the limited association and never substitute that party into the GRA snapshot. New product aliases need provider evidence in addition to the GRA listing. The build validator rejects incomplete records but cannot perform the human source review.
